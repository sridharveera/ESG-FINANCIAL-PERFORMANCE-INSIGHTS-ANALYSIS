# ESG & Financial Performance Insights Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes financial performance, ESG performance, and environmental indicators across industries and years using an integrated data analytics workflow.

The objective is to provide data-driven insights through interactive Power BI dashboards covering financial performance, ESG metrics, and environmental impact.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Financial and ESG metrics are often reviewed separately, making it difficult to assess overall business and sustainability performance.

This project integrates financial, ESG, and environmental indicators into interactive dashboards to support comparative analysis across industries, regions, and years.

## 📈 Key Insights

### Financial Performance

Technology records the highest total revenue in the dataset at approximately 16.48M and also has the highest average profit margin at approximately 18.80.

### ESG Performance

Finance records the highest average ESG Overall score among the nine industries, at approximately 64.6.

### Environmental Performance

Energy records the highest average carbon emissions at approximately 8.19M and also records the highest energy consumption and water usage totals in the dataset.

### ESG Trend

Average ESG Overall score increases from 51.4 in 2015 to 57.8 in 2025.

## 📌 Project Limitations

- The analysis uses secondary data rather than primary company data.
- Environmental metrics do not have standardized measurement units in the source dataset.
- 2015 GrowthRate values are blank because previous-year values are unavailable.
- The analysis identifies patterns and comparisons but does not establish causal relationships.
- Aggregated dashboard results may not represent every individual company-level difference.
